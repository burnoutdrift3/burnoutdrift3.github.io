Wordle Unlimited

"Wordle Unlimited" brings the worldwide word guessing sensation to your browser with zero daily limits! Guess the hidden 5-letter word in six tries using green, yellow, and gray feedback clues.

Description  Wordle Unlimited

  • Unlimited Daily Puzzles: Play as many word puzzles as you want—never wait 24 hours again.
  • Intuitive Color Feedback: Green for correct letter and spot; yellow for correct letter in wrong spot.
  • Comprehensive Dictionary: Recognizes thousands of valid English five-letter words.
  • Stat Tracker: Monitor win streaks, guess distributions, and average attempts over time.

How to Play:

  • Enter 5-Letter Word: Type letters using your physical keyboard or the on-screen virtual keyboard.
  • Submit Guess: Press Enter to submit your guess; each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • Analyze Clues: Green indicates correct placement; yellow means letter exists elsewhere; gray means not in word.
  • Solve in 6 Tries: Narrow down letter options strategically to discover the secret word.
