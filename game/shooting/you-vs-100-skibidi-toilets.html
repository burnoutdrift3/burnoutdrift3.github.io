You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets

"You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets" You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets pits you against a relentless horde of 100 singing toilet monsters. Equip heavy military weapons, dodge attacks, and purge the city.

Description  You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets

  • Survive Against 100 Toilets: Fight off waves of quirky singing toilet enemies swarming you from every direction.
  • Heavy Combat Firepower: Unleash explosive RPGs, miniguns, AK-47s, tactical shotguns, and fragmentation grenades.
  • Titan Boss Toilets: Take down giant Cameraman and G-Man toilet bosses equipped with high health and laser beams.
  • Hero Upgrades: Upgrade movement speed, armor defense, weapon damage, and magazine size between rounds.

How to Play:

  • Move & Sprint: Use WASD keys to move, Shift to sprint away from toilet swarms, and Space to jump.
  • Aim & Fire: Aim crosshairs with mouse, Left Click to fire, and Right Click to zoom with weapon sights.
  • Switch Weaponry: Use number keys 1-6 or mouse scroll wheel to switch to heavier weapons for big crowds.
  • Target The Heads: Score critical damage by shooting directly at the heads emerging from the toilets.
