Editorial Guidelines & Review Policy

How we evaluate, benchmark, rate, and document every HTML5 browser game. We operate under strict principles of transparency, player safety, and editorial independence.

Policy Version: 2.1 (October 2026) Audited for Google E-E-A-T Compliance

Our Core Editorial Principles

At Burnout Drift 3, trust is our most valuable asset. Millions of browser gaming fans and students rely on our portal to discover engaging, responsive, and secure games. To safeguard this trust, our editorial team adheres to three foundational rules:

  1. Uncompromising Player Safety: We will never publish any game containing malware, hidden miners, deceptive download links, or intrusive adult content.
  2. Hands-On Verification: We do not publish generic automated game feeds. Every single title in our catalog is physically played and tested on modern desktop and Chromebook hardware by a human editor.
  3. Editorial Independence: Advertisers and game creators have no veto power or editorial influence over our game ratings, controls guides, or performance evaluations.

The 5 Criteria for Featuring a Game

Before any title is added to our racing, drifting, or casual library, it must achieve a passing grade across our 5-pillar scorecard:

  • 1. Frame Rate & Frame Pacing (Weight: 25%): Games must sustain an average of 50-60 frames per second on low-tier integrated graphics (e.g., Intel UHD 600 series) with minimal frame drops.
  • 2. Controls Responsiveness (Weight: 25%): Input latency from key press to screen action must stay under 50ms. Drifting titles like Burnout Drift 3 require immediate steering and handbrake response.
  • 3. Network & Firewall Resilience (Weight: 20%): Assets must load reliably without triggering generic web filter alarms or requiring external binary plugins.
  • 4. Game Loop & Progression (Weight: 15%): The game must have clear progression, upgrade mechanics, or high-replayability gameplay loops.
  • 5. Local Storage Save Stability (Weight: 15%): Progress, high scores, and car modifications must persist accurately across browser restarts via HTML5 LocalStorage.

Corrections, Updates & Broken Game Reporting

Browser environments and game engines change frequently. If a browser update causes a game frame to load blank, or if a developer pushes a broken patch, our team takes prompt corrective action:

  • Real-Time Monitoring: We periodically run automated iframe health checks to catch 404s and asset loading failures.
  • Community Reports: Users can flag broken games via our Contact Page. Reported game issues are investigated and triaged within 24 hours.
  • Changelog Transparency: Significant revisions to gameplay guides, controls layouts, or game versions are documented directly on the relevant page.

Advertising & Commercial Disclosure

To provide completely free access to all players with no mandatory fees, Burnout Drift 3 displays standard display advertisements served through reputable networks such as Google AdSense.

We enforce strict ad quality standards:

  • No auto-playing sound ads that disrupt classroom or library environments.
  • No deceptive "Download Here" or "Play Now" banner spoofing that tricks players into downloading third-party software.
  • All commercial placements are clearly differentiated from game frames and editorial content.
