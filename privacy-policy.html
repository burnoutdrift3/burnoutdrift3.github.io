Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At Burnout Drift 3, we prioritize user anonymity and safety. We collect zero personally identifiable information (PII) from casual players and adhere strictly to global child online safety regulations.

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 COPPA / GDPR / CCPA Compliant

Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Our Commitment to Young Players: Burnout Drift 3 does not require user registration, passwords, full names, addresses, or phone numbers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

Because many of our visitors play from school or community networks, we enforce strict ad network filtering to block sensitive categories and prevent targeted profiling of minors. Game state saving is handled exclusively via client-side storage on your own device.

Information Collection & Technical Logs

When you access burnoutdrift3.github.io, our servers and third-party partners (such as Google Analytics and GitHub Pages infrastructure) automatically receive standard non-identifying technical data:

  • Browser & OS Information: Browser type, version, screen resolution, and operating system (used to verify WebGL compatibility).
  • Network Logs: Anonymized IP addresses (truncated), referring URLs, and page visit duration.
  • Game State (LocalStorage): Your car tuning settings, unlocked tracks, and high scores are saved locally inside your browser's localStorage container. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Cookies & Google AdSense

We partner with Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements that keep our website completely free for everyone.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your geographical location, you have statutory rights concerning your digital privacy:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request confirmation of whether any personal data has been processed.
  • Right to Erasure: You can clear your game saves and cached cookies at any time by clearing your browser's local browsing data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We never deny service or degrade game performance for users exercising privacy rights.

For any privacy questions or data inquiries, please email our Data Privacy Officer at support@burnoutdrift3.github.io.
