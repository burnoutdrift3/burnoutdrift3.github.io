Who We Are

Founded in 2023, Burnout Drift 3 (burnoutdrift3.github.io) started with a singular, clear goal: provide racing and drifting enthusiasts with an instant, lag-free web gaming experience that runs effortlessly anywhere, including restrictive network environments such as school campuses and shared computer labs.

Unlike bloated game aggregator portals packed with disruptive video popups, invasive redirects, or unplayable Flash conversions, we focus exclusively on lightweight, modern HTML5 and WebGL titles. Every game in our catalog is personally play-tested, verified for compatibility, and optimized to run with native keyboard and gamepad support.

Our Philosophy: Gaming should be accessible, safe, and immediate. We believe players deserve instant entertainment without risk to their personal devices or privacy.

Our 4-Step Game Testing Methodology

To maintain our reputation for excellence and adhere to Google's rigorous E-E-A-T guidelines, every game published on our portal undergoes a strict four-phase verification pipeline:

1

Security & Sandboxing

Static code inspection to ensure no malicious scripts, crypto miners, or illicit tracking pixels exist within game bundles.

2

Hardware Benchmarking

Testing on low-spec hardware (Intel Celeron, 4GB RAM Chromebooks) to guarantee consistent 55-60 FPS performance.

3

Unblocked Reliability

Verification that games load cleanly through school firewall proxies without relying on blocked CDN domains.

4

Controls & Usability

Manual playtesting by our editorial staff to document accurate keyboard shortcuts, game tips, and save reliability.

Respecting Developers & Intellectual Property

We hold immense respect for game creators and indie developers. All games featured on our portal are hosted via publicly authorized developer embeds or standard open web distribution licenses. We never modify underlying game source code, remove developer watermarks, or disrupt built-in developer revenue models.

If you are a copyright holder or game studio wishing to update, feature, or request the removal of your game, please visit our DMCA / Copyright Policy or contact our compliance desk directly at dmca@burnoutdrift3.github.io. We process all verifiable requests within 24 business hours.
